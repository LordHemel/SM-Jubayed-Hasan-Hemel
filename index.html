Projects

Croc Royale — 4-Player Online Battle Royale

A 4-player online crocodile battle royale built solo in Unreal Engine 5.8 with Blueprints, from rig to packaged online build in about 10 weeks (Aug–Oct 2026). Players host or join over Epic Online Services, hunt AI prey to grow, fight with five crocodile attacks and survive a shrinking zone on a 2 km map; the last croc alive wins.

Highlights: listen-server multiplayer with server-authoritative combat, RepNotify and multicast replication; a 29-bone crocodile rigged in Blender with 40+ animations generated by Python scripts; an island map generated from code (Python heightmap and layout, Unreal Python builder); and optimisation from a 966k to 14.5k triangle player mesh, reaching about 60 FPS on an Intel Arc A750.

Unreal Engine 5.8 Blueprints Redpoint EOS Multiplayer Blender + Python Solo Dev

Greenhand — A Peaceful Farming Journey

A full 3D first-person farming simulator built solo in Unity 6 (URP). Features include a complete plant growth system with watering and fertilizing mechanics, 7 unlockable plant beds, 2 crop types, a customer queue and selling system, day/night cycle with sleep mechanics, tool upgrade system, NavMesh-based customer AI, and a full JSON save/load system.
